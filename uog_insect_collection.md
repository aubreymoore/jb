# Online catalog for the University of Guam Insect Collection

The online catalog for the University of Guam Insect Collection was previously hosted by the Symbiota Collections of Arthopods Collection (SCAN).
Unfortunately, SCAN was retired during 2025 due to lack of funding. 

This post is intended as a way to share notes on an attempt to find a new home for the online catalogue.

One of the great features of SCAN was automatic upload of data to the Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF).
GBIF facilitates public access to global biodiversity information. The [University of Guam Insect Collection Dataset](https://www.gbif.org/dataset/56e311e3-43c6-4b99-aa21-af396074d5e3)
was last updated on May 19, 2023 and it contains data records associated with 34,527 specimens in the insect collection.
These records can be searched online and/or downloaded in various formats.
