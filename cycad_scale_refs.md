# Cycad scale references

Here is a very short list of references relevant to biological control of *Aulacaspis yasumatsui* (CAS) the cycad scale insect which has caused an ecological disaster on Guam. This insect has killed more than 90% of the island's endemic cycad, *Cycas micronesica*, which was the most abundant tree in Guam's forests prior to arrival of CAS in 2003.

- [2022 review article on CAS biocontrol](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aubreymoore/CAS-biocontrol-seminar/main/Cave-Moore-Wright-2022-CAS-Biocontrol.pdf)
- [2022 report from Ron Cave documenting his consulting visit to Guam](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aubreymoore/CAS-biocontrol-seminar/main/Cave-2022-Guam-CAS-report.pdf)
- [Marler et al (2026). Genetics, survival, and demographic decline of *Cycas micronesica* due to invasive insect species on an oceanic island](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44353-026-00089-9)
