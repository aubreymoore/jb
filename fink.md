Do you ever have a word pop into your head out of nowhere?

Just happened to me while enjoying a sunset Margareta on the deck. A little voice inside my head whispered the word "fink". Got no idea where this came from. Perhaps a cosmic ray hit a neuron in my brain and caused a misfire as
I wasn't wearing my aluminum foil hat at the the time. Within milliseconds other neurons fired and I remembered that this word was in use way back in the 1970s. I remember Grandad was sure this was a nasty swear word and forbade its utterance in his presence and I remembered the phrase "the king is a fink".

So what was a fink and why don't we hear about finks anymore? I thought I'd look into this just for fun.

I quick [Google search for "fink etymology"](https://share.google/aimode/OkjuXfNccjkrLWtGI) returned: 

"Fink" (noun: snitch/strikebreaker; verb: to inform) is American slang from the early 1900s, likely originating from German, meaning a "finch" or "loose/dissolute person."

Digging deeper, within a few minutes I had a [timeline for the word's usage](https://books.google.com/ngrams/graph?content=fink&year_start=1930&year_end=2008&corpus=en-US-2012&smoothing=3) thanks to a wonderful online tool called the Google Books Ngram Viewer. As you can see. if you bother to click on [the friggin' link](https://books.google.com/ngrams/graph?content=fink&year_start=1930&year_end=2008&corpus=en-US-2012&smoothing=3), the peak usage for "fink" is about 1970. But Ngram provides more info. If you scroll down, you can search [Google books from 1967 t0 1970](https://www.google.com/search?q=%22fink%22&tbm=bks&tbs=cdr:1,cd_min:1967,cd_max:1970&lr=lang_en). When I click on the link, the second book reference is entitled [The King is a Fink](https://www.amazon.com/King-Fink-Wizard-ID/dp/0449137090) by Brant Parker and Johnny Hart who were responsible for [the Wizard of Id](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wizard_of_Id) daily newspaper comic. Always one of my faves.

Still trying to find the reason for the demise of finks. If I find anything further, I will report it here.
